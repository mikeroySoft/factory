# Prospective pilot protocol addendum — 2026-09-10

Status: frozen before replacement enrollment and before any eligible candidate or model call. This addendum supersedes only the points below in `PROTOCOL.md`. The initial 2026-09-10T21:50:58Z enrollment selected no candidate and is preserved under `enrollments/20260910T215058Z-superseded/`.

1. **Handoff snapshots.** Handoff bytes are stored once by content hash. Each observation has a separate immutable metadata record keyed by the corroborating event ID/line hash (or a single content-hash poll key). Identical bytes observed for worker `result`, `exit`, classic `attempt`, and approval therefore cannot collide or overwrite different provenance.
2. **Acceptance binding.** An earlier unbound `APPROVE` is insufficient. Acceptance must bind one current exact head across: a modern recorded review (`accepted=true`, `head=actual_head`), the later `approved` row (`head=gate_head=review_head`, integer PR), the selected worktree HEAD, and captured PR identity/head. Review and approval must share ticket and dispatcher run. Any intervening worker outcome or later `REVISE` invalidates the review binding. Missing or mismatched fields fail closed.
3. **Plan-context status is a proxy, not approval.** Closed/`ready-for-agent`/`ready-for-human`/`factory-approved` context status only admits a candidate for capture. It does not prove that the referenced item is an approved downstream plan. Before model trials, Main must manually qualify from the frozen pre-outcome bytes that the reference really is an approved downstream plan/consumer and that the task is ordinary implementation work. This review cannot inspect handoff content or depend on its usefulness. Rejection makes the selected case ineligible and ends the one-case enrollment; it is not replaced.
4. **Continuation.** On a completely bound accepted case, the observer remains supervised in `READY_FOR_CONTEXT_REVIEW` instead of exiting or making model calls. Six trials remain held until Main records the context qualification and independently reviews setup. Before that external prerequisite, `READY/WAITING` is the complete result. No generic runner is added while no case is enrolled.
5. **Smoke scope.** The observer check must drive a full synthetic append/tick containing same-byte worker `result`, `exit`, classic `attempt`, bound `review`, and bound `approved`, and must prove an accepted source/handoff freeze without metadata collision. Direct helper-only capture is not sufficient.

The replacement enrollment binds the SHA-256 of both protocol files. The model-isolation overlay is also frozen locally and its six effective settings are checked through `PI_CONFIG_FILES` plus `omp config get`; `--config` remains the future trial-process overlay. These checks invoke no model and do not change user/global configuration.
