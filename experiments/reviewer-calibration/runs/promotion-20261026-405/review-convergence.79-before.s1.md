- `snapshot()` calls `feedback.collect` for every selected ticket with an `agent/*` PR, one after another. `GRAPHQL` fetches up to 100 PRs in any state, and `selected_issue` keeps every ticket that has a PR, so merged and closed PRs are included.
- Each `collect` runs at least 6 `gh` calls (two head reads, reviews, threads, check runs, statuses). Its 30-second limit applies to each PR separately; nothing bounds the whole snapshot.
